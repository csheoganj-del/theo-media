# TheoMedia SEO: The Next 90 Days Execution Roadmap
**Goal:** Establish consistent organic inbound enquiry flow for bespoke web design across the UK and Ireland.

---

## Month 1 (Days 1–30): Foundation, Indexing & Local Citations

### 1. Indexation Monitoring & Coverage
- Monitor Google Search Console weekly for URL indexation across all 45 routes.
- Track Core Web Vitals in Google Search Console: ensure 100% of URLs maintain &ldquo;Good&rdquo; status on mobile (LCP < 2.5s, CLS < 0.1, INP < 200ms).

### 2. Local Directory Citations (NAP Consistency)
Register accurate, consistent Name, Address (or Service Area), Phone (`+353 85 225 8004`), and Website URL on premier UK & Irish business directories:
- **UK Directories:**
  - Yell.com (UK leading directory)
  - FreeIndex.co.uk
  - Scoot.co.uk
  - Thomson Local
  - The Independent Design Directory
- **Irish Directories:**
  - GoldenPages.ie
  - YourLocal.ie
  - Chambers Ireland Business Directory

### 3. Google Business Profile Optimization
- Complete profile setup as a Service Area Business (SAB) covering Greater London, Manchester, Edinburgh, Dublin, Cork, and Galway.
- Add primary category: &ldquo;Website Designer&rdquo;.
- Add secondary categories: &ldquo;Internet Marketing Service&rdquo;, &ldquo;Software Company&rdquo;, &ldquo;Graphic Designer&rdquo;.
- Upload high-resolution screenshots of the 6 live prototypes (Cinder & Field, Velora House, etc.).

---

## Month 2 (Days 31–60): Case Study Velocity & Industry Outreach

### 1. Digital PR & Hospitality Outreach
- Pitch editorial commentary to UK & Irish hospitality trade publications (e.g. *The Caterer*, *Hospitality Ireland*, *Eat Out Magazine*) based on our insight piece: *&ldquo;The True Cost of PDF Menus and Third-Party Commission Leakage for Independent Restaurants&rdquo;*.
- Publish thought-leadership summaries on LinkedIn highlighting before/after mobile load speed comparisons between Squarespace and custom Next.js builds.

### 2. Add Live Client Showcase Studies
- As new commercial clients launch, produce detailed 1,200-word case studies documenting:
  - Client initial challenge (slow speeds, platform lock-in, high monthly retainer)
  - Architectural solution (Next.js, Tailwind, direct API integration)
  - Tangible commercial results (e.g., +38% mobile reservation completion, 99/100 Core Web Vitals score)

---

## Month 3 (Days 61–90): Review Acquisition & Keyword Expansion

### 1. Review Velocity Architecture
- Establish an automated post-launch client feedback workflow requesting reviews on:
  - Google Business Profile (highest weight for local ranking)
  - Trustpilot or Clutch.co profile
  - Video or written testimonial for the TheoMedia homepage

### 2. Programmatic Regional Expansion (UK & Ireland)
Based on Search Console query impressions from Month 1 and 2, evaluate launching targeted regional sub-pages under clean URL structures if justified by search volume:
- `/web-design-london`
- `/web-design-manchester`
- `/web-design-dublin`
- `/web-design-edinburgh`

### 3. Conversion Rate Optimisation (CRO)
- Review analytics on `/pricing`, `/industries/restaurant-website-design`, and `/contact`.
- Test hero headlines and WhatsApp CTA engagement rates to maximize enquiry conversions per 100 visitors.
