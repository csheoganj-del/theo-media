Converting Carts to Customers: Why Friction Kills Sales.

E-commerce success is a game of friction. Every extra second a product page takes to load, or every confusing input field during mobile checkout, drops conversion rates by up to 20%. Clunky off-the-shelf plugins slow down your database and lead to abandoned checkouts.

TheoMedia designs and develops streamlined ecommerce platforms tailored to UK and European consumer habits: one-tap Apple Pay and Google Pay checkouts, VAT-ready automated invoicing, instant product search filters, and direct WhatsApp communication channels that keep shoppers informed and coming back.
