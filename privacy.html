PRIVACY & ENQUIRIES

What happens when you contact us.

This page explains the current website enquiry flow. Contact TheoMedia at hello@theomedia.co.uk with questions about your information.

The enquiry form

The form uses your name, email address, selected package and project description to prepare an email in your own email application. Completing the form does not submit these details to a TheoMedia website server. You must send the prepared message from your email app for us to receive it.

Email and WhatsApp

When you send us a message, we receive the information you include so we can discuss your enquiry. Please include only information relevant to your project, and do not send passwords, payment details or sensitive personal records.

Email and WhatsApp operate through their respective service providers. Their handling of information is also subject to their own privacy policies.

Website connections

This website loads fonts from Google Fonts and an animation library from cdnjs. Your browser connects to these providers to request those files. Hosting and these services may process technical request information, such as your IP address, to deliver the website.

Questions about your information

To ask how your enquiry information is used or retained, or to request access, correction or deletion, email hello@theomedia.co.uk.
